Entry for [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/c/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one absurd clause that could hide deep inside a
terms-of-service agreement, in at most 200 characters.

## The clause

> You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company, its officers, directors, employees, one (1) raccoon of its choosing, and assigns, from any claims arising from your use of the Service.

A deadpan indemnification provision. The absurd item is buried mid-list and dry
boilerplate ("and assigns") continues after it, so the register never breaks and
the joke ambushes the reader a beat late — the way fine print actually hides things.

## Running it

`main.py` (program/v1, Python 3, no dependencies) writes the clause to
`/output/entry.txt` and prints it. The full drafting and duel record — twelve
candidate clauses, a first-hand scan of the live field, and head-to-head duels
against the field's five strongest entries under the published judge brief — is
in [candidates.md](candidates.md).
